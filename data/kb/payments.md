# Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard and PayPal. Cash on delivery is available in Nairobi only.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, no money is taken and the order stays unpaid for 2 hours so you can try again. If M-Pesa deducted money but the order shows unpaid, contact support with the M-Pesa confirmation code.
