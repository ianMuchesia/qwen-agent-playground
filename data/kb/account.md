# Your account

## Resetting your password
Tap "Forgot password" on the login screen, or ask support to send a reset link to the email on your account. Reset links expire after 1 hour.

## Changing your email
Go to Settings, then Account, then Email. We send a confirmation link to the new address before the change takes effect.

## Deleting your account
Account deletion is permanent. Ask support to delete your account; it is completed within 7 days and any open orders must be finished first.
