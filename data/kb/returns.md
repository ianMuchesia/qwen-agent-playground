# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return an item within 30 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packaging. Returns after 30 days are not accepted, except for damaged items.

## Refunds
Refunds are processed within 5 business days after we receive the returned item. The money goes back to your original payment method, including M-Pesa.

## Damaged items
If an item arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours with photos. We will send a free replacement or a full refund, and you do not need to pay return shipping.
