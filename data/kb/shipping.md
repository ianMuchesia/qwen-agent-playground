# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard delivery within Kenya takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery within Nairobi takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed after 2pm ship the next business day.

## Shipping costs
Shipping is free on orders over KSh 5,000. Below that, standard shipping costs KSh 300 and express shipping costs KSh 600.

## International shipping
We ship to Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda. International delivery takes 7 to 10 business days and customs fees are paid by the customer.

## Tracking an order
Every order gets a 4-digit order number. You can track it in the app under My Orders, or ask support with your order number.
